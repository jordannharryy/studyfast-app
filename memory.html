Before you read

Set your intention.

Your brain retains what it is looking for. This takes 60 seconds and changes everything you remember from the next reading session.

Jordan: Most people open a document and just start reading. Their brain has no filter, so it treats every sentence as equally important. Nothing stands out. Nothing sticks. Set your intention first and your brain becomes a heat-seeking missile for what matters.
1. Why are you reading this?
Learn something new
Solve a specific problem
Get a quick overview
Find one key idea
Prepare to teach it
Make a decision
2. What do you already know about this topic?
Even a sentence. This primes your existing knowledge so new information connects to something.
3. What is the one question you want answered?
One specific question. Your brain will hunt for the answer automatically as you read.
Your intention is set

The single most powerful memory technique in existence. Retrieval is the memory, not the reading.

Jordan: Roediger and Karpicke proved this in 2008. Retrieval practice beats re-reading by a significant margin every time. Writing what you remember right now does more for retention than reading the document twice.
